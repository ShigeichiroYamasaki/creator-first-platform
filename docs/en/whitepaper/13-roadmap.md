# 13. Roadmap

## Evidence-gated delivery

The roadmap advances by acceptance evidence, not calendar promises. A failed legal, rights, security, usability or cost gate blocks the affected capability without silently lowering the requirement.

## Phase 0 — foundation

Publish the whitepaper, charters, terminology, CFP process, ADRs, protocol specifications, test requirements and decision register. Resolve material open questions and identify accountable reviewers.

## Phase 1 — music MVP and public experiment

Validate the smallest end-to-end journey: account, passkey or wallet binding, creator profile, synthetic or licensed test content, Navidrome playback through a gateway, MockJPYC subscription, supporter credential and observable usage evidence. Polygon Amoy participant funding is capped and pre-approved as described in [Chapter 12](/en/whitepaper/12-infrastructure-cost).

The MVP does not prove production rights clearance, sustainable economics, official JPKI verification, production stablecoin settlement, censorship resistance or decentralised governance.

## Phase 2 — creator economy

Pilot rights review, collaborator confirmation, versioned splits, creator-centred allocation, community support and treasury reconciliation with a small, contractually authorised cohort. Evaluate retention, creator benefit, dispute workload, fraud and operating cost.

## Phase 3 — verifiable platform

Add reproducible usage commitments, independent calculation checks and, where justified, transparent zero-knowledge proofs. Exercise bicameral governance, timelocks, emergency handling and upgrade evidence.

## Phase 4 — production readiness

Build production independently from testnet. Complete legal and tax structuring, rights contracts, approved-asset review, privacy assessment, security audits, key-management rehearsal, service-level and capacity tests, accessibility review, support operations and board approval. Any STO remains a separate regulated workstream.

## Success criteria

Success means demonstrable creator benefit, convenient and trustworthy user experience, lawful rights and payment operations, understandable governance, bounded cost and recoverability. Token issuance, transaction count or testnet participation alone is not success.
