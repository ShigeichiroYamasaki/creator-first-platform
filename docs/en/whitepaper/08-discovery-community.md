# 8. Discovery and Community

## Discovery principles

Discovery should preserve user autonomy, give new and niche creators meaningful opportunity, expose the logic of major ranking modes and resist manipulation. The service should offer plural modes such as personal relevance, editorial context, community playlists, local scenes, deliberate serendipity and popularity with visible controls.

Popularity and discovery opportunity are separated. A discovery budget can reserve exposure for eligible works without guaranteeing outcomes. Cold-start treatment relies on declared context, diverse exploration and measured feedback rather than fabricated engagement.

## Supporter relationships

A user can express support for a creator from the player. With explicit consent, the platform may issue a non-transferable Supporter SBT. If the governed early-tier condition is met, the credential can indicate Early Supporter status. The current proposal uses support registration within one year of the creator's initial registration, but this rule remains governed and versioned.

Credentials can gate non-financial community privileges such as access to a forum, advance information, participation badges or bounded voting eligibility. They must not imply copyright ownership, profit share, repayment, guaranteed access forever or investment return. Revocation, expiry, wallet recovery, privacy and anti-Sybil rules are required.

## Community safeguards

Community playlists, curators and scene-based groups should expose sponsorship and conflicts. Reputation must be contestable and should not become an irreversible social score. Moderation combines published rules, proportionate action, human appeal and records suitable for audit without exposing victims or private listening histories.
