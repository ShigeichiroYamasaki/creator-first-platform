# 7. Governance

## Two houses

The Creator House contributes rights, production and creator-economy expertise. The User House contributes usability, affordability, privacy and audience interests. Eligible members are selected through verifiable sortition for limited terms. A non-transferable membership credential may display current office, but the authoritative entitlement is the governed registry and can expire or be revoked.

Sortition inputs, eligibility snapshots, randomness, selection algorithm, exclusions and results must be reproducible. Representation corrections may be used only through published, bounded rules.

## Deliberation and voting

A CFP begins as a natural-language proposal with scope, motivation, alternatives, impact, constitutional analysis and acceptance criteria. Evidence, objections, amendments, minority views and conflicts of interest are recorded in versioned minutes. AI may summarise or simulate scenarios, but must identify sources and uncertainty; it has no vote and cannot replace accountable reviewers.

Quadratic voting may express preference intensity within a capped credit budget. It is not used as a shortcut around eligibility, quorum, bicameral approval or constitutional limits. Both houses approve according to their own quorum and threshold rules.

Natural-language intent is linked to requirement IDs, test IDs, source commits, build artefacts and deployed bytecode. Independent and accountable reviewers evaluate tests; legislators approve the evidence package and policy decision, while implementers, auditors, directors and service operators retain responsibility for their own acts.

## Constitutional and corporate limits

Law, binding contracts and the three charters constrain ordinary resolutions. A court order, rights dispute, security emergency or directors' statutory duties can block execution. The block, reasons, authority and remediation path must be recorded. Emergency powers are narrow, time-limited and retrospectively reviewed.
