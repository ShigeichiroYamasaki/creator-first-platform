## Terminology

“Music creator” means an independent, artist-direct artist who is not controlled by a major label and makes material decisions about creation, distribution and marketing. “User” means a listener or participant using the service. The “Creator House” and “User House” are the two proposed deliberative chambers. “CFP” means a Creator First Proposal.

## Relationship to protocol specifications

The whitepaper explains purpose and policy. Accepted governance decisions and Architecture Decision Records explain design choices. The [protocol specifications](/en/protocol/) convert those choices into requirements and tests. Code is not accepted merely because it was deployed: its source, build, tests, approvals and deployed bytecode must remain traceable.
